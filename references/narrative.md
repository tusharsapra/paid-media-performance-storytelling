# Narrative and outputs

Lead with the business answer. Use observation → evidence → interpretation → implication → action. Write plain English, concrete metric names and concise comparisons. Select material findings rather than reciting every column. Preserve unfavorable material facts and honest unknowns.

Say “reached 120% of target” and “used 80% of planned budget.” Say “CPA fell 15%” when direction matters. Distinguish percentage points from relative change. Put material caveats beside claims and keep source file/sheet/row references in evidence notes.

Weekly: outcome statement, two to four material changes/exceptions, immediate actions and blockers. Monthly: scorecard, period movement, product/funnel/market/channel contributors, relevant creative/audience findings, changes, outcome quality and next-period priorities. Executive summary: main outcome, strongest driver, material unknown and priorities.

Slide copy: evidence-based title, two to four points, useful chart suggestion and source/qualification note. Do not create a full deck when only insights were requested.

Action tracker: scope, proposed action, reason/evidence, owner/proposed role, dependency, proposed/committed timing and success measure. Question coverage: each ask, answer/reference, unresolved evidence and next check; use internally unless useful to the reader.

## Fictional example

For complete, comparable and equally mature cohorts, previous spend is 90,000 with 450 leads and 180 qualified leads; current spend is 80,000 with 600 leads and 180 qualified leads. Current plan is 100,000 spend and 500 leads.

“The campaign delivered 600 leads, reaching 120% of target while using 80% of the planned media budget. Cost per lead fell 33.3% to 133.33. However, qualified-lead volume remained at 180 as the qualification rate fell from 40% to 30%. Acquisition became cheaper, but the extra leads did not increase qualified demand. Review quality by campaign and audience before expanding spend.”

Do not claim qualified-lead cost stayed flat: it fell from 500.00 to 444.44. Do not invent a channel driver or qualified-lead target. These values and currency are fictional.

Before delivering, verify arithmetic, definitions, period/target versions, sources, evidence strength, action consistency and question coverage. Keep requested short formats concise while retaining material qualifications.
