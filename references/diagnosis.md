# Paid-media diagnosis

Choose the business objective first. Separate volume, efficiency, quality and plan delivery; inspect segments and allocation mix before explaining aggregates.

| Pattern | Investigate | Checks |
|---|---|---|
| More outcomes with proportional spend | Similar average efficiency at higher volume | Quality, recent trend and available scale. |
| Lower CPA but missed volume plan | Efficient but insufficient delivery | Phasing, constraints and demand. |
| Spend rises while outcomes fall | Efficiency deterioration | Tracking, lag, mix, bids, intent and landing page. |
| CTR improves while downstream results weaken | Click response without business delivery | Placements, audience, intent and page journey. |
| Cheaper clicks but dearer qualified outcomes | Potentially weaker traffic quality | Cohort maturity, duplicates and sales acceptance. |
| Platform outcomes up, business outcomes flat | Source-specific growth | Definitions, attribution and coverage. |
| Frequency up, CTR down, CPA up | Possible saturation/fatigue | Creative age, audience mix and concurrent changes. |
| Blended CPA down, segment CPAs stable | Allocation mix effect | Spend weights and segment quality. |
| Lowest CPA on few outcomes | Promising but uncertain result | Exposure, variance, maturity and test design. |
| Search delivery falls | Inventory or eligibility change | Demand, status, rank, budget and query coverage. |
| Lost share due to rank | Rank-related restriction | Bids, relevance and page experience; budget alone may not fix it. |
| Better diagnostic scores | Better diagnostics | Validate actual business results. |
| Promotion lifts attributed ROAS | Revenue-efficiency movement | Discounts, refunds, margins and timing shifts. |

Use these as investigation pathways, never automatic optimisation instructions.

Separate brand/non-brand, acquisition/remarketing and business competitors/auction participants. Preserve format-specific click/video-view definitions. Confirm country coverage within region labels. Include material weaknesses beyond requested priority markets. Keep unmapped delivery visible and paused campaigns' historical results in scope.

Match creative comparisons by objective, market, audience, placement, period and source. Lower CTR can coexist with lower CPA. Unequal algorithmic delivery is observational evidence, not a randomised creative experiment.

Awareness: use the agreed reach, exposure, quality-traffic or lift objective; cheap clicks alone do not prove impact. Lead generation: assess qualified progression on comparable cohorts. E-commerce: separate paid orders, new customers, revenue, refunds, failed payments and margin. Subscriptions/apps: separate trials/installs from paid or retained users. Publishers: compare commitments and validated delivery, duplication and quality.

Choose maintain, investigate, repair measurement, revise, test, scale cautiously, reduce or pause. Include scope, reason, roles, dependency, review timing and success measure. Check demand, quality, saturation and strategic role before scaling; do not invent an exact budget percentage.
